From the Great Commentary of the I Ching (Zhouyi · Xici I):

> The metaphysical is called the Way; the physical is called the vessel.

A "vessel" (qi) is a being defined by its form — a cup can only hold water,
a qin can only make sound. Confucius said, "A junzi is no vessel." It is
often read as praise for the generalist. One layer deeper, it is a
resistance to **being defined**.

## Buqi Is Not Omnipotence

Buqi does not mean mastery of every art. A master of everything can still be
defined by the very word "generalist." The original sense of buqi is this:
to refuse absorption into any fixed shape, and to keep alive the possibility
of becoming something else.

## Buqi in the Age of Algorithms

A recommender system is the most powerful vessel-making machine there is:
it defines you from your history, then feeds that definition back to you,
until you really do grow into that shape.

Seen, not defined — harder today than two thousand years ago, and for that
very reason, more worth keeping.
