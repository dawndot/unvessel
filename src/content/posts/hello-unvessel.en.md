This is the first post on unvessel (Buqi, 不器).

**Seen, not defined.** The first half commits me to keep writing — being seen
presupposes visible traces. The second half commits me to refuse every
ready-made template — not being defined means this site owes no one an
"ought to look like."

## Why Black

Night knows no daylight. Pure black `#070707` is the stance of this territory:
content glows upon it, the way cinnabar rises through ink-black thought.

## Why Giant Type

The two characters 不器 are blown up to viewport scale because they deserve
to be gazed at — the I Ching says, "The metaphysical is called the Way;
the physical is called the vessel." Junzi buqi: to be a person of virtue is
to refuse conscription by any single shape.

## How This Site Runs

It is built with Astro, rendered fully static. More unusually, it keeps a
complete operations interface for AI: a post is one markdown file, and a CLI
plus MCP tools handle scaffolding and validation. The post you are reading
may itself be a trace of human–machine collaboration.
